Simple Dialogues (includes 1-3 constructs)
1. Dialogue: "Show me just any hotel."
Query: Hotel
2. Dialogue: "I am looking for a camping site next to the open sea or a lake."
Query: Camping_Site and (nextTo some (OpenSea or Lake))

Difficult Dialogues (includes 4 or more constructs)
   1. Dialogue: "Find me an accommodation in Italy where I can bring my dog, and it should be close to both a forest and a lake."
Query: Accommodation and (inCountry value Italy) and AllowsPets and (nextTo some (Forest and Lake))
   2. Dialogue: "I need a hostel with free Wi-Fi in Germany, near public transportation, and within walking distance to an art gallery or a museum."
Query: Hostel and (inCountry value Germany) and HasFreeWiFi and (near some PublicTransport) and (walkingDistance some (ArtGallery or Museum))

The examples above include some common logical constructs such as and, or, some, and value. However, you are not limited to these. You can also incorporate other constructs like not, exactly, sameAs, or hasValue, or any others that align with the requirements of your ontology and dialogues. Use whichever constructs make the most sense for effectively representing and reasoning with your data.
